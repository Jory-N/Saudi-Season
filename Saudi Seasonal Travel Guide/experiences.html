Different Experiences

Saudi Arabia offers many different experiences. Discover what you can do based on your interests.

Heritage & Culture

Discover the history and culture of Saudi Arabia through historical sites, traditional markets, and old neighborhoods.

  • Explore Hegra in AlUla
  • Visit Diriyah in Riyadh
  • Walk through Al-Balad in Jeddah
  • Visit Al-Qaisariya Souq in Al-Ahsa

Nature

Enjoy Saudi Arabia's natural landscapes, from green mountains and valleys to beautiful oases.

  • Explore the mountains of Abha
  • Visit Al-Ahsa Oasis
  • Enjoy the natural scenery of Al-Baha
  • Discover the landscapes of Taif

Sea & Beaches

Relax by the sea and enjoy coastal destinations with beautiful views and different water activities.

  • Walk along Jeddah Corniche
  • Explore the Red Sea coast
  • Visit Al-Khobar Corniche
  • Enjoy water activities and beaches

Adventure

For adventure lovers, Saudi Arabia offers exciting outdoor activities in deserts, mountains, and natural areas.

  • Hike through mountain trails in Abha
  • Explore the desert landscapes of AlUla
  • Try outdoor activities in Al-Baha
  • Discover different hiking locations around the Kingdom

City Experiences

Experience modern Saudi cities through shopping, restaurants, entertainment, and famous landmarks.

  • Explore Riyadh's modern attractions
  • Visit restaurants and cafés in Jeddah
  • Enjoy the waterfront in Al-Khobar
  • Discover shopping and entertainment destinations
