Explore The Cities

Every season reveals a different side of Saudi Arabia. Discover the best destinations to visit throughout the year.

Winter

Enjoy cool weather, cultural attractions, heritage sites, and beautiful outdoor destinations.

  • Riyadh — Experience city life, cultural attractions, and winter activities.
  • AlUla — Explore ancient heritage sites and breathtaking desert landscapes.
  • Al-Ahsa — Discover the oasis, traditional markets, and historic landmarks.
  • Al-Khobar — Enjoy the waterfront, city attractions, and pleasant winter weather.
  • Jeddah — Explore the Red Sea coast, Al-Balad, and cultural attractions.

Spring

Spring is perfect for enjoying nature, mountain views, and pleasant outdoor weather.

  • Al-Baha — Discover green mountains, forests, and peaceful outdoor spaces.
  • Taif — Enjoy blooming roses, mountain views, and pleasant weather.

Summer

Escape the summer heat by visiting Saudi Arabia's cooler mountain destinations.

  • Abha — Experience cool temperatures, mountain scenery, and green landscapes.
  • Al-Baha — Relax among forests, mountains, and refreshing summer weather.

Autumn

Autumn brings comfortable weather for coastal trips, city experiences, and outdoor exploration.

  • Al-Khobar — Enjoy the waterfront, beaches, and relaxing coastal atmosphere.
  • Jeddah — Discover the Red Sea, historic Al-Balad, and the city's coastal atmosphere.
